● The Clipra roadmap

Good things are in motion.

Clipra is in development. Here is what we are planning for the first release and exploring for later. Features may change as we test.

INSIDE THE BUILD

Calm, intentional clipboard software.

We are building a clipboard manager for Mac, iPhone, and iPad, with automatic organization, text tools, and private iCloud sync.

Current Phase: In development
SwiftUI + CloudKit Core
Planned for v1.0

Smart Auto-Sort

Rules will sort copied links, color codes, and code snippets into pinboards. Custom rules will let you organize by content or source app.

Target: macOS Menu Bar • iOS Keyboard Companion
Planned for v1.0

Text Transformer

Clean tracking parameters from links, format JSON, change letter case, encode or decode text, and remove formatting. Transformations are planned to run on your device.

Status: Planned for the first release
Privacy

Private iCloud sync

The planned design stores history locally and syncs through your private iCloud database. Clipboard content will not be stored on AR Swift servers.

Planned for Pro and Lifetime
Mobile Ecosystem

iOS Custom Keyboard Extension & Dynamic Island

A custom keyboard will provide access to saved history in supported apps. A Share Sheet extension will let you save items on iPhone and iPad. Background clipboard capture is not part of the iOS plan.

Platform: iPhone • iPad • Dynamic Island Status
Backup & presentation tools

JSON backups & Screen Share Hide

Export and import history as JSON on Mac. Screen Share Hide is planned to keep the clipboard panel out of view during presentations and recordings.

Status: Planned for Mac
