A few good questions

Good to
know.

Here's what to know about how Clipra is being built.

Which devices will Clipra support?

Clipra is planned for Mac, iPhone and iPad. The initial iPad experience will use the iPhone layout, with iPad-specific refinements planned for a later phase.

How will clipboard sync work?

The planned architecture uses CloudKit and the user's private iCloud database. Local history should remain available when a device is offline, with sync happening in the background.

Can iPhone apps monitor the clipboard in the background?

No. iOS restricts background clipboard access. Clipra is planned to provide history access through its custom keyboard extension and Share Sheet integration.

What makes Clipra different?

Smart Auto-Sort and Text Transformer are the two central differentiators: content-aware rules organize copied items, and built-in tools transform text before you paste it.

Is Clipra available to download now?

Clipra is in development. The website currently presents planned features and pricing; App Store and Mac download links will be added when the product is ready.
