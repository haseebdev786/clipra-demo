THE ROADMAP · V1.0 FEATURES ARE PLANNED

Inside the build

From copy chaos to a calmer workflow.

We're shaping Clipra around two ideas: your clipboard should organize itself, and the things you copy should be ready to use in whatever form you need.

PROJECT STATUS V1.0 IN DEVELOPMENT
V1.0PLANNED
CORE DIFFERENTIATORSMART ORGANIZATION

Smart Auto-Sort

Your clipboard should know where things belong. Clipra is planned to recognize links, email addresses, color codes, code snippets and files — then route them into the right pinboard automatically. You’ll also be able to create custom rules, like “anything copied from Figma goes to Design Assets.”

POWER TOOLTEXT TRANSFORMATION

Text Transformer

Copy once, then shape the result to fit the job. Planned tools include uppercase and lowercase, Title Case, URL tracking cleanup, JSON prettify and minify, Base64 encode and decode, plain-text cleanup, word count and URL encode/decode.

CORE EXPERIENCEMAC · IPHONE · IPAD

Private iCloud Sync

Start a copy on one device and find it on another. Clipra's planned sync layer uses CloudKit and your own private iCloud database. Local-first writes keep the interface responsive while sync runs in the background.

MOBILE EXPERIENCEIOS EXTENSIONS

Your clipboard, right on the keyboard

On iPhone and iPad, Clipra is planned to bring saved history into a custom keyboard extension, with a Share Sheet action for saving content from apps like Safari and Messages. iOS limits background clipboard access, so the keyboard and share extension are key parts of the experience.

PRIVACY & CONTROLYOUR RULES

Privacy rules that respect your space

Choose which apps Clipra should ignore, protect access with Face ID or Touch ID on iOS, and hide the Mac clipboard shelf during screen sharing. The goal is simple: useful history without exposing things that should stay private.
